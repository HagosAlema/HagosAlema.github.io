Technical Architecture & Signal Pipeline

PulseCheck converts subtle fingertip tissue brightness variations under camera torch illumination into precision cardiac inter-beat interval streams entirely on-device.

Camera rPPG & Uniform Resampling

Captures raw green-channel frame brightness via CameraX. Because frame delivery rates jitter, signal frames are resampled onto a strict uniform temporal grid before digital filtering to eliminate phase distortion.

CameraX Uniform Resampling Kotlin

Zero-Phase Bandpass Filtering

Filters raw brightness signals forward and backward to cancel phase delay shifts, preserving the exact timing of systolic peaks required for accurate RMSSD calculation.

Filtfilt DSP Pure Kotlin

Parabolic Sub-Sample Peak Interpolation

Fits quadratic parabola curves over discrete 30 fps sample points, achieving sub-millisecond inter-beat interval precision (<0.02 ms error compared to 60 fps capture).

Sub-sample Precision Quadratic Fit

Spectral Purity & Artifact Rejection

Validates signal confidence by evaluating spectral power density alongside local median artifact rejection, refusing to output misleading numbers under motion noise.

Spectral Density Artifact Rejection
